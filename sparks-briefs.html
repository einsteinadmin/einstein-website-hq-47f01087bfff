Sparks Briefs — the 9 survivors

Motion & delight ideas that survived Cameron’s 7/8 vote (15 cut). These are specs, not builds — each says what the moment is for, where it lives, and what it must never do. Cameron’s standing note: “curious… not a full endorsement yet.” Nothing gets built without a discussion. House rules travel with every brief: reduced-motion = static final state · no fake numbers · never gate content behind motion.

Already shipped 7/8: the CTA jiggle (hero + sticky “See my price” wiggle every ~7s, motion-safe) — Cameron ask, mirrors the live site. Live on the homepage mockup now.

⭐ 1 · The Receipt Printer

Home: pricing page honesty section or awards review wall · Effort: medium · Tier 1 set piece

The job: make “we come in under the quote” a felt experience — a receipt printer scroll-prints real review lines with real dollar figures (“Quoted $840. Final bill $712.”). 49 under-the-quote reviews with dollar figures already mined from the Google corpus.

Behavior: prints 3–5 receipts on scroll-in; each is a verbatim quote + reviewer first name/last initial + branch. Kicker: “We keep the receipts. So do they.”

⚠ Cameron 7/8: must work as a tap on mobile, not hover. ⚠ Every quote word-for-word real — the component IS a receipts claim; one punched-up quote breaks the whole device.

⭐ 4 · The Chalkboard Proof

Home: candidate = the killer-stat coverage block (homepage) · Effort: medium-high (real illustration/animation) · Tier 1

The job: a chalk equation works itself out as you scroll — quote + stairs + dresser + weekend, each term appearing, resolving to the answer.

The merge opportunity (discuss first): this may be the killer-stat visual. Einstein chalks the industry’s real math — 5 lbs × 60¢ = $3 — stares at it, crosses it out, writes “= what it’s actually worth.” One build closes two open items, and chalk-math is the most on-brand motif we own (concept #5 on the killer-stat visual ballot).

⚠ Needs real illustrated frames — this is a KP art deliverable, not a CSS trick. Reduced-motion: final chalkboard state, static.

10 · Late-Night Line Swap

Home: homepage hero subhead · Effort: low · Tier 2 delight

The job: between 10pm–5am the hero subhead swaps to a night variant naming the current time — “It’s {11:42 PM}. Still packing? We should talk.” Rewards the anxious midnight researcher — which is exactly who's up quoting movers.

⚠ Copy must stay empathy-register, never guilt. No urgency/scarcity framing (VP tripwire adjacency). Fallback: standard subhead.

11 · Four Saplings

Home: origin-story P.S. line (homepage) or About · Effort: low-medium · Tier 2

The job: scroll-in draws four small saplings for “four trees planted for every move,” slow patient easing, then a counter settles. Quiet, warm, one-time.

⚠ Counter value must be real/maintained or omitted — no fake-live numbers. SVG line-draw, cheap on mobile.

12 · Badges on Nails

Home: awards badge wall (page + homepage pattern) · Effort: low-medium · Tier 2

The job: award badges hang on illustrated nails; hover/tap gives one small physically-honest pendulum swing (2–3° max, damped). Makes the “trophy shelf” literal without clutter.

⚠ Pairs with the badge-wall display decision (grid vs marquee) — nails suit the grid; the marquee wants no per-item motion. Real badge art is now wired into the mockups (7/8), so this can be feel-tested cheaply.

13 · Honest Placeholders

Home: quote-form fields (KP’s build) · Effort: low · Tier 2

The job: form fields rotate deadpan placeholder text per focus — address: “The one with the stairs you forgot to mention.” Humor at friction points = the form’s own doctrine, so this is the spark most native to the form’s register.

⚠ Lives in the working conversion tool — ship behind a flag, watch completion rates. Never joke on the payment/contact steps.

⭐ 14 · 404: “This Page Moved.”

Home: site 404 · Effort: low · Tier 3

The job: the 404 page is a moving box under a bare bulb: “This page moved. Ironically, nobody hired us.” CTA back home. The joke only visitors in trouble ever see — cheap, zero risk, pure brand.

Needs one illustration. Candidate for the first thing KP designs, as a warm-up.

17 · Bubble-Wrap Strip

Home: below the footer · Effort: medium · Tier 3

The job: scroll past the footer to a strip of 40 poppable bubbles (sound OFF by default). Deadpan counter: “You popped 12. We understand.” Rewards the people who read to the very bottom.

⚠ Pure toy — keep it below the legal footer so it never competes with conversion. Tap targets ≥40px. No sound without explicit opt-in.

18 · Print = Receipt

Home: pricing page print stylesheet · Effort: low · Tier 3

The job: printing/save-as-PDF on the pricing page outputs a clean receipt-formatted one-pager instead of the webpage — the “we keep the receipts” brand made literal for the spreadsheet-comparison shopper.

Pure CSS @media print. Zero risk to the page. The one-pager must carry the same claims discipline (ranges labeled, no committed rates).
