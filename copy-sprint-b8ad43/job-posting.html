Job posting: your old friend.

You rewrote this thing in 2022 and it carried us for years. It's drifted. Time for round two.

The setup

The mover posting you rewrote in 2022 (your doc, still in the vault) has been touched by a lot of hands since. It still works — but the seams show, and the numbers around it got serious: mover cost per hire is north of $450, and the applicant funnel leaks at every step from impression to hire.

Conversion copy is the cheapest attack on that number we have. Better ad spend costs money; a posting that makes the right person stop scrolling costs a rewrite.

The ask

Rewrite the mover / lead mover posting top to bottom. Not a punch-up — a rewrite. You wrote the original voice; you get to decide what survives.

  1. The WARNING-style opener is yours to keep, kill, or replace with something better. No sacred cows.
  2. Keep the pay transparency. Specific dollar ranges are strategy, not bravado — "competitive compensation" hand-waving is the thing this posting exists to not do.
  3. Keep the honest physical reality. "You'll sweat, a lot" self-selects the right people in and the wrong people out. That's the funnel working, not leaking.

Current live copy (verbatim)

One stale number in here: "Inc. 5000 list every year for the last 6 years" — we're an 8x Inc. 5000 winner now. We restamp counts on our side; you never need to verify a number, just write around it.

The posting — as captured from the live listing (North Dallas version; pay numbers vary by branch)

WARNING: This will be the best job post you read all day.

Actually, why don't you just go ahead and make it the only job post you read all day, mkaaaayy? *sips coffee*

Here's the deal: We're a moving company. We pick up people's stuff and carry it from one place to a different place.

Sounds simple enough… but it turns out that to be REALLY GOOD movers (the only kind worth being, in our opinion), we need REALLY GOOD people for our North Dallas branch.

People like you. ;)

And because really good people like you deserve to be paid well and treated with respect, we put together this pretty kickass compensation & benefits package:

What's In It For You

DURING TRAINING — $24.00 to $26.50 an hour: A typical trainee makes between $24.00 and $26.50 an hour ($18 base wage + performance bonuses and tips).

ONCE OUT OF TRAINING — $26.50 to $31.25 an hour: A lead mover typically makes between $26.50 and $31.25 an hour ($19.50 base wage + performance bonuses and tips).

Speaking of pay... we pay straight time for hours worked. From clock in to clock out, all hours count the same towards how much you make! No need to worry about billable vs non-billable hours affecting your true hourly rate.

On Call Pay — Additional $5 an hour whenever you are called into work last minute.

W2 Status (Not 1099) PLUS Great Benefits

  • Worker's Comp to cover you if you get hurt on the job
  • Affordable healthcare (including company contributions towards premiums) and dental insurance through BlueCross BlueShield
  • Vision benefits and company sponsored short term disability insurance through Mutual of Omaha
  • Advancement Opportunity — 100% of our managers started as movers, and we're growing fast!
  • Good People & Great Culture — There's a reason why people don't want to leave once they start working here. We've got fun team events and outings (that you'll actually want to come to), and a practice of recognizing great work.

Minimum Requirements

  • Ability to lift, carry, push and/or pull heavy objects (up to 125 lbs overhead, 200 lbs independently, 500 lbs in coordination) frequently throughout an 8+ hour day
  • Availability to work at least 4 days a week, of which at least 2 are weekend days (Thursday-Sunday)
  • Must be 21+ years of age
  • Valid driver's license, clean driving record with at least 2 years driving experience (CDL not required)
  • Well rounded: customer service skills, strong initiative, excellent communicator, team-oriented, loves to learn
  • Ability to pass a criminal background check, drug screen, and physical

Proof Points

  • Inc. 5000 list every year for the last 6 years
  • Video testimonials from current team members
  • Reviews on Yelp, Angie's List, Google, Facebook
