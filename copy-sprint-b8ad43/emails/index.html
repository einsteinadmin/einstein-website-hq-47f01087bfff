Where to spend the time

The big four, in order:

  • Booking confirmation — the highest-volume email we send. Two variants exist today (a full version and a shorter auto-confirm); if you think they should be one email, say so.
  • Meet Your Movers — lands a few days before the move, at peak customer anxiety. This is the warmth-matters-most email.
  • The post-move review ask — short, high-volume, and it feeds the review engine everything else brags about. We also need a text-message version (2–3 sentence options) — the current SMS lives in a third-party tool and honestly needs a fresh start anyway.
  • The lead-rescue pair — "no availability" and "we tried calling you" are where warm leads go to die. Punchy beats polite here.

The quote email itself is done — the finished doc above is your style target, not an assignment. Give it a once-over; if a line bugs you, flag it. Your writing time goes to the seven below. The post-kickoff confirmation is the caboose: worth a trim if there's time, fine as-is if not.
