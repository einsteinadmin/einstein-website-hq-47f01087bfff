📦 Packing Tips
  • The BEST thing you can do to prepare for your move is to check off all the items on our Smart Move Checklist
  • How to pack your dresser: Most dressers weren’t designed to be moved with the extra weight of clothes and other items. So for the safety and structural integrity of your furniture, please remove and pack all items — especially tiny and valuable things like glasses and jewelry! Often, furniture needs to be tilted or even turned over to be moved safely, and we don’t want your items to fall out, get lost, and go on a Toy Story-esque adventure.
  • Follow the 3 rules of packing:
    1. Boxes with fragile items should be clearly marked on multiple sides.
    2. Never skimp on the padding.
    3. When in doubt, carry it separately.
    Want more tips? Check out The Smart Guide to Packing.
  • Moving something valuable? Please mark the box or item very clearly, and alert your awesome movers.
  • We’re happy to hook up appliances, but… we’re not trained plumbers! If you need help connecting your appliances and/or securing water lines, we’re happy to help — but you’ll need to sign a liability waiver and either complete the final inspection yourself, or hire a plumber to do it.

🤙 No Worries Damage Coverage
Here at Einstein, we lead the moving industry in transparency, service, and being generally rad. As an extra form of protection for your home and belongings, our hourly rate includes what we call "No Worries Damage Coverage".
This valuation coverage allocates up to $2,000 per move to repair, replace, or provide a cash settlement for items damaged during the move process.
There are just a few exceptions to this policy, though:
  • Particle Board Furniture: While we do our best to move particle board furniture without damage, the inherent weakness of its design causes problems. Therefore, we cannot offer additional coverage beyond the $.60/lb required by TXDMV for damages to any particle board furniture (i.e. we only cover $.60 per pound for these items). For more, check out TXDMV’s "Your Rights and Responsibilities When Moving In Texas".
  • You Pack It, We Move It: All items packed by the owner are moved at the owner’s risk. Therefore, we cannot offer additional coverage beyond the $.60/lb required by TXDMV for damages to items packed in boxes by the owner (i.e. if something breaks in a box you packed yourself, we are only liable for up to $.60 per pound). For more, check out TXDMV’s "Your Rights and Responsibilities When Moving In Texas".
  • Other Things We’re Not Liable For: When we’re not driving the transportation vehicle (e.g. a POD or rental truck), or we’re unable to use our own methods of protection (e.g. when we can't use our own pads in a storage unit).
