Already ruled — don't re-litigate these

These tweaks are banked for the reprint. They've been argued, decided, and closed — build on top of them, not against them:

  • QR + offer line move to the FRONT of both versions (the offer is currently exiled to the back).
  • A selling caption goes under the crew photo — highest-readership real estate on the card, currently blank.
  • Smart Scheduling pillar: "On time. Every time." → "Booked in minutes. Crews that show when they said." (The old line is an absolute one traffic jam falsifies.)
  • Back-side sub gets rewritten — "That's $300+…" points at a table the reader hasn't reached yet; the new version will give the pointer an antecedent.
  • "Nine years straight" restamps to ten. Austin made it ten while the card was in the mail.
  • The TV Boxes row is being replaced with appliance disconnect/reconnect.

Guardrails

Three things with tripwires attached:

  1. The "$0 Trip/Fuel Fee" row is true only because of where we mail. The zip lists deliberately exclude trip-fee zones. That claim never travels to any other channel — not the website, not a billboard, not an email — and any wording that generalizes it beyond this table gets cut, not debated.
  2. The TxDMV legal block is untouchable. It's a state requirement, not copy.
  3. Award counts, streaks, and review numbers get restamped by us, not you. Write around them; don't burn time verifying. (For orientation: review counts are market-scoped — 1,340+ was Austin at print; the company-wide number is a different, bigger one.)
