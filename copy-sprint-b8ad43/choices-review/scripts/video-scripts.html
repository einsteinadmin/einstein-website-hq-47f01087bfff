CHOICES Training Videos

Scripts & Storyboards for the Hybrid Training Portal

📅 Created: February 2026 ⏱️ Total Runtime: ~5-6 minutes 🎬 4 Video Segments

Video Overview

Video Title Duration Purpose
Video 1 Welcome 1:30 - 2:00 Personal intro, set expectations, build connection
Video 2 Why Culture Wins 2:30 - 3:00 Luling story, Einstein's track record, "culture can't be copied"
Video 3 CHOICES Intro 0:45 - 1:00 Quick acronym reveal, hand off to interactive scenarios
Video 4 Congratulations 0:30 Welcome to the team, next steps (optional)

Video 1 Welcome

1:30 - 2:00
Goal Build personal connection. Make them feel welcomed, not processed. Set up why this training matters without being preachy.

Opening Shot

📹 VISUAL
Cameron on camera, casual setting (office or warehouse background with Einstein branding visible). Relaxed, conversational energy—like talking to a new team member, not presenting to a boardroom.
🎤 CAMERON (ON CAMERA)
0:00 Hey! Welcome to Einstein Moving Company. I'm Cameron, and I'm genuinely excited you're here.

0:08 Look, I know what you're probably thinking: "Great, another corporate training video." I get it. But this one's different—I promise.

0:18 See, at Einstein, we've figured something out. We're not the only moving company with trucks. We're not the only ones with strong people and good equipment. So how do we keep winning Best of Austin, Best of Texas, year after year?

0:32 (slight pause, leaning in)

It's not what we do. It's how we do it. And that's what this training is really about.
Setting Expectations
🎤 CAMERON (ON CAMERA)
0:45 Here's the deal: the next 20 minutes or so, you're going to learn the 7 values that define how we work. But you're not going to learn them by watching me talk—you're going to learn them by experiencing real scenarios that our movers face every single day.

1:00 You'll make choices. You'll see what happens. Some of it might even make you laugh. (smile)

1:08 By the end, you'll understand exactly what separates an Einstein mover from everyone else. And honestly? If you made it through our hiring process, I already know you've got what it takes.

1:20 So let's get into it. First up: why culture matters more than anything else we do.

📝 Production Notes

  • Keep energy warm and conversational—not salesy or corporate
  • The "another corporate training video" line should feel self-aware and genuine
  • Consider a subtle smile or eyebrow raise at "make you laugh"
  • End with forward momentum—they should be curious about what's next

Video 2 Why Culture Wins

2:30 - 3:00
Goal Convince them that culture is THE thing. Use the Luling story to make it memorable. Show (don't just tell) Einstein's results. Land the "culture can't be copied" insight.

The Luling Story

📹 VISUAL
Cameron on camera. Consider cutting to photos/footage of small-town Texas, cross-country runners, or simple graphics showing the population number.
🎤 CAMERON (ON CAMERA)
0:00 Let me tell you about a tiny town called Luling, Texas. Population: about 5,500 people. That's it. One high school. Nothing special on paper.

0:12 But here's the thing: their cross-country team has won multiple state championships. Year after year, they beat schools that are ten times their size.

0:24 Now, can they recruit the best runners from across the state? No—kids are born there or they're not. Do they have better facilities? Nope. Special coaching secrets? Not really.

0:38 So how do they keep winning?

(beat)

Culture. That's it. They've built a championship culture where excellence is just... what you do. It's the expectation. It's contagious.
Einstein's Results
📹 VISUAL
  • Cut to B-roll: Einstein trucks, crews loading/unloading, real moves in progress
  • Show award badges animating on screen: Best of Austin (8x), Best of Texas (3x)
  • Flash Yelp/Google 5-star ratings
  • Show real review quotes if available
🎤 CAMERON (VOICEOVER / ON CAMERA)
0:55 That's exactly what we've built at Einstein.

Eight-time Best of Austin. Three-time Best of Texas. 5-star ratings across the board. Over 100,000 moves and counting.

1:10 And look—I'm proud of those numbers. But what I'm really proud of is how we got there.
Culture Can't Be Copied
📹 VISUAL
Cameron back on camera. This is the key insight—delivery should be direct and confident.
🎤 CAMERON (ON CAMERA)
1:20 Here's something I want you to really get:

Our competitors can buy the same trucks we have. They can match our pricing. They can copy our website, our marketing, even license the same technology.

1:35 But there's one thing they absolutely cannot copy: our culture. The way our people show up. The choices they make when no one's watching.

1:48 That's our competitive advantage. And that's what you're about to learn.

1:58 We call it CHOICES.

📝 Production Notes

  • The Luling story should feel like you're letting them in on something—not lecturing
  • B-roll during the "Einstein's Results" section keeps energy up and provides visual proof
  • The "culture can't be copied" section should feel like a genuine insight, not a corporate slogan
  • "CHOICES" at the end is a cliffhanger—leave them wanting to know more

Video 3 CHOICES Intro

0:45 - 1:00
Goal Quickly reveal the CHOICES acronym. DON'T explain each value in detail—that's what the scenarios are for. Build excitement for the interactive portion.

The Reveal

📹 VISUAL
  • Cameron on camera briefly
  • Cut to animated CHOICES graphic (the one with icons for each value)
  • Each letter/value can highlight briefly as he says the acronym, but don't linger
🎤 CAMERON (ON CAMERA → VOICEOVER)
0:00 CHOICES. Seven values that define how we work.

0:05 (as graphic displays)
Communicate clearly. Hustle but don't rush. Ownership over results. Improve every day. Conscientious attention to detail. Elevate attitudes. Support one another.

0:22 (back on camera)
Now, I could spend the next hour explaining each one of these. But honestly? That's not how you learn.

0:30 You learn by doing. So here's what's about to happen: you're going to face seven real scenarios that Einstein movers deal with all the time. You'll make a choice, and you'll see exactly what happens—good or bad.

0:45 Ready to see what you're made of? Let's go.

📝 Production Notes

  • This video should be QUICK. Resist the urge to explain each value.
  • The acronym read-through can be fast—they'll see the graphic and can reference it later
  • "See what you're made of" should feel like a fun challenge, not a test
  • End with energy and momentum—they're about to click into the interactive portion

Video 4 Congratulations

0:30
Goal Celebrate their completion. Make them feel like part of the team. Point to next steps. Keep it short and genuine. (This video is optional—the portal has a completion screen regardless.)

Welcome to the Team

📹 VISUAL
Cameron on camera. Warm, celebratory energy. Maybe Einstein mascot or branding visible.
🎤 CAMERON (ON CAMERA)
0:00 Hey—you made it! Seriously, well done.

0:05 You now know the seven values that make Einstein movers different. But here's the real question: which one is your superpower? And which one is your growth edge?

0:16 Talk to your manager about your People Analyzer worksheet. Be honest with yourself. That's how you get better.

0:24 Welcome to Einstein. Let's get to work.

📝 Production Notes

  • Keep this genuinely brief—they just finished 20+ minutes of training
  • "Let's get to work" should feel energizing, not exhausting
  • This video is optional—consider whether it adds value or if the on-screen completion is enough
