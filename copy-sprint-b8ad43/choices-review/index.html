Good to Know

What to Expect

So now you know the values. Here's how you'll actually see them show up.

You'll hear CHOICES constantly.

In team meetings, in shout-outs, on the walls. It's the language we use here. When someone does something great, we name the value. You'll pick it up fast.

Expect feedback tied to values.

Good or constructive, your manager will connect feedback to a specific value. "That's A-Player Ownership" or "Let's talk about what Hustle looks like in that situation." It's how coaching works here.

Everyone leads by example. Including you.

You don't need a title to set the tone. If you see something that needs doing, handle it. If a teammate's struggling, jump in. First week or fifth year — same expectation.

We check in. For real.

Surveys, reviews, honest conversations. We pay attention to how the culture is doing and make tough calls when someone isn't living up to it. This training isn't a one-and-done — it's day one of how we operate.

Bottom line: you're not just learning these values today and moving on. You're joining a team that lives them.
