Two kindergarten friends. One old pickup truck.

That was 2012. A hundred thousand moves later, it’s 10 branches across Texas and Tampa — and the top of the org chart is still two guys from the same kindergarten class.

Our story is your standard “two best friends meet in kindergarten, grow up together, and decide to start a moving company.” Oh, you’ve never heard that one before? Well, it’s a classic around here. [live copy, verbatim]

The longer version: Cameron Brown and Paul Morin grew up best friends, roomed together at Texas A&M, and took a few swings after graduating — a nutritional supplement company, a Christmas tree business — while working part-time for a moving company to pay the bills.

That part-time job is where they saw it: the moving industry was clunky, outdated, and deserved its bad reputation. Hidden fees. Coverage that paid 60¢ a pound. Crews nobody vetted. Everyone in the industry knew — and nobody was fixing it.

So in 2012, with an old pickup truck, a small business loan, and a lot of determination, they set out to build the moving company they’d actually want to hire. 50/50 from day one, built from scratch — no franchise, no buyout, no shortcuts.

Every part of the Smart Move System traces back to that list.

🌳 Four trees, every move.

Moving uses trucks, fuel, and a small mountain of cardboard — so for every move we complete, we plant four trees with our partner Eden: People + Planet — local crews paid to plant every one. Not a marketing gimmick — we put back more than we take. It’s how we’re going carbon neutral, one move at a time. Move by move, that adds up to hundreds of thousands of trees in the ground.
