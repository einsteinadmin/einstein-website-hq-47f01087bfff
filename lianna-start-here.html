Where this project is

The structure and facts are largely settled; the words are why you're here. We've mocked ten pages in working HTML, and the copy was drafted in-house — the bones are right, but a lot of it needs the thing only you do: voice, punch, and a de-slopifying pass.

Design is moving in parallel. Our agency (Kickpoint — Emma on design) is running their design pass on this same set right now, so treat the visuals as scaffolding: placeholder photos, dummy phone number, layouts that will keep shifting. Don't spend a single note on how anything looks. The words are the target.

Two guardrails: First — every number and claim on these pages is a verified fact (review counts, coverage dollars, award tallies, the 60¢-per-pound industry standard). Punch the sentences around them however you like; the numbers themselves don't move. Second — headlines have mostly been through the wringer already and are close to locked, so the highest-value real estate for you is everything below the fold: leads, body copy, transitions, CTAs, microcopy.

How to leave notes

Every page above has a feedback layer built in. No accounts, no setup:
  1. Highlight any text on a page → a 💬 button pops up → your note attaches to that exact copy.
  2. 💬 pins sit on each section for bigger-picture notes ("this whole block is trying too hard").
  3. When you're done — on any page — open the blue Feedback button (bottom-left) and hit "Copy ALL feedback." That grabs every note from every page in one paste. Drop it in an email back to Cameron.

Notes save in your browser, so do the whole review on one device. Rewrites bigger than a note fit fine too — just paste your version into the note itself.

What we're NOT asking for (yet)

Round two is our email sequences — quote follow-ups, booking confirmations, review asks. Website first. And if partway through you decide this is day-rate-shaped, rolling-work-shaped, or burn-it-down-shaped, say so — you'll have a better read on the scope than we do once you've seen it.
