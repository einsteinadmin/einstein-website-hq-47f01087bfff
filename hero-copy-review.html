Hero Copy Review — the 3-question test

Every drafted page's hero, graded against Harry Dry's copy test + the hero checklist. Your workshopped copy is always Option 1 — nothing gets swapped without you picking it. Where the original already passes, I say so and recommend keeping it.

1 · Can you see it?Concrete nouns and scenes, not abstract benefit words. "1,000 songs in your pocket," not "5GB of storage."
2 · Can you falsify it?Claims that could be proven wrong read as credible. "Best" and "premium" are free to say, so they're worth nothing.
3 · Could a competitor say it?If they could paste your headline on their site and it holds, it's filler. The line must be ownable.
How to use this: per page — read the verdict, tap the option you want (tap again to un-pick), add a note if you have one. Picks save in this browser. When you're done, hit "Copy ALL decisions (for Albert)" at the bottom and paste it to me. Grading detail lives in each card; the scoreboard below is the 10-second read.
