2 · Navigation — two proposals

Current mockup nav (7 links + CTA) has no FAQ and no dropdowns. The live site solves discovery with dropdowns; the mockups haven’t needed them until now. Both proposals below add FAQ and give Locations Cameron’s all-cities-visible dropdown. Ballot on the cut list.

N1 — “About absorbs the company pages” Albert rec
🧠 EinsteinPricingCoverageServices ▾ 6 servicesLocations ▾ all 10 citiesWhy UsReviewsFAQAbout ▾ story · team · careers · blog · contactSee My Price

8 links + CTA. FAQ earns top-level (it’s a pre-booking objection-killer, not company trivia). Reviews stays top-level — it’s the trust page and likely an SEO keeper. About becomes the drawer for company/infrastructure pages (story, our team, employment, resources, contact) — which matches how the live site already clusters them under /about-us/. Trade-off: 8 links is one more than the mockups carry today; fine on desktop, and mobile collapses to the hamburger anyway.

N2 — “Trust cluster”
🧠 EinsteinPricingCoverageServices ▾Locations ▾ all 10Why Us ▾ why us · reviews & awards · our story · our teamFAQSee My Price

6 links + CTA — tighter than today’s mockup nav. All trust/company pages cluster under a Why Us dropdown. Trade-off: Reviews and About lose top-level visibility, which may fight Elizabeth’s SEO guidance and buries the awards page that KP wants as a reusable pattern. Cleaner bar, weaker scent trail.

⚑ For Elizabeth before locking either: which of these pages need top-level nav placement for SEO (internal-link equity), and do the live URLs (/frequently-asked-questions/, /about-us/reviews/) keep their paths in the rebuild?
